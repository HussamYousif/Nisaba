## Hershey vector fonts (`scribe/src/main/resources/hands/*.json`)
Glyph geometry derived from the Hershey Fonts, digitized by Dr. A. V. Hershey (U.S. National Bureau of Standards).
The Hershey Fonts are in the public domain, subject to the simple restriction that their origin notice is retained:

> USE RESTRICTION: This distribution of the Hershey Fonts may be used by anyone for any purpose, commercial or
> otherwise, providing that: (1) the following acknowledgments are retained — "The Hershey Fonts were originally
> created by Dr. A. V. Hershey while working at the U.S. National Bureau of Standards."; (2) the format used here is
> not the NTIS distribution format.

Extracted with the `Hershey-Fonts` Python package (MIT, © 2020 apshu) by `tools/scripts/export_hershey.py`.
Hands: casual = `futural` (print-like, most legible), formal = `cursive`.
